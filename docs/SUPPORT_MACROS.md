# Support macros — Coins / Orders / chargebacks

Paste into Admin CS replies or email. Keep tone short and factual.

## Coins balance missing after pay

> Thanks for writing. Paid Coins credit when Stripe Checkout returns you to calabi with a successful session. Please reply with: (1) the email on your calabi account, (2) approximate time/amount, (3) Stripe receipt email if you have it. We will match the Payment and credit the ledger if the return page was closed early.

## Orders tab empty

> Orders under Profile → Coins → Orders lists Coin pack purchases and spends on this account. If you just paid, open Coins → Orders after the Stripe return page finishes. Still empty? Send the Stripe receipt id and we will reconcile.

## Duplicate charge

> We can refund a duplicate Checkout in Stripe if both sessions show captured. Reply with both receipt ids / last4 and amounts. Do not buy another pack until we confirm.

## Chargeback / dispute opened

> We received a card dispute for this payment. Related Coins may be reversed and creator payouts held until Stripe closes the case. If this was accidental, please withdraw the dispute with your bank and tell us — we can restore access faster.

## Escrow request tip not released

> Request tips stay held until the creator marks the request done and Admin releases. If it has been more than 14 days with no fulfillment, we refund the donor. Share the live channel and approximate time of the tip.
